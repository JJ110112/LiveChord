FAQ

Why donate? I can already use it for free, right?

Exactly — staying free is the LiveChord promise. This page is for users who want to say thanks or who want to help keep the project alive. The cost of a coffee is enough to run the GPU for two extra days, which adds up.

Can I get a refund?

Buy Me a Coffee treats donations as one-off gifts, so refunds aren't enabled by default. If there's a serious misunderstanding, email hiteacherwu@gmail.com within 7 days and I'll handle it manually.

Can you issue a receipt or invoice?

Not currently — this is an individual project, so no formal invoice or tax receipt. Buy Me a Coffee sends an email confirmation that can serve as a personal donation record. For corporate sponsorship, partnerships or commercial licensing, please email me.
