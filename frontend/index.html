How LiveChord works

  1. 1

    Upload your audio

    Drag an audio file onto the upload area or click to pick one. MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A and OGG up to 200 MB. The file lives in memory only for the duration of analysis — it never goes into a long-term archive.

  2. 2

    AI analyzes the song

    A chord-recognition transformer detects the chord progression. A beat-tracking model finds bar lines and tempo. A section detector labels verses, choruses and bridges. Typical analysis takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes depending on song length.

  3. 3

    Practice with synced views

    The player opens with chords, fingering and timing in lockstep with the audio. Transpose to your singing key, slow playback to 0.5×, loop a single section A-B, switch instrument view at any time. Built for actual practice — not just casual listening.
